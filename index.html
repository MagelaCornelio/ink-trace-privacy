Ink Trace

Last updated: 2025-08-04

Ink Trace ("the app") respects your privacy. This policy describes what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Data we collect
  • Email address and name (when creating an account with email or Google Sign-In)
  • Your imported books, reading progress, quotes, highlights, and reviews are stored locally on your device
  • We do not collect location data, contacts, or any additional personal data
How we use your data
  • Your email is used solely for authentication through Supabase
  • All your reading data stays on your device and is not sent to external servers
  • We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties
Data storage
  • App data is stored locally using Isar Database
  • Authentication is handled securely through Supabase
  • You can delete your account and all associated data at any time
Your rights
  • You can delete your account from settings
  • Uninstalling the app automatically deletes all local data
  • You can contact us to request deletion of your email from our authentication system
Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at:
